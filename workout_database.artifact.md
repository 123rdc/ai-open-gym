# Workout Database Expansion Artifact

## Overview
This artifact documents the massive expansion of the Gym Form Coach workout database. The database has been expanded to include a comprehensive list of exercises for every major body part, ensuring a wide variety of training modalities.

## New Categories
- **Shoulders**: Added as a primary body part with a full set of exercises.
- **Training Modalities**: Every body part now includes variations for:
    - Barbell / Free Weights
    - Dumbbells
    - Machines
    - Cable Variations
    - Bodyweight / Calisthenics

## Expansion Highlights
- **Total Exercises**: Increased from ~36 to **112** exercises.
- **Exercises per Body Part**: Every body part now features **16+ exercises**.
- **Visuals**: High-quality Unsplash image URLs updated for all exercises.
- **Detail**: Enhanced "About" sections for better form guidance.
